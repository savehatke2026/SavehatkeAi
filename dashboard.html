How Refunds Work
Refunds are created automatically from verified payments.
1
Automatic detection
When your payment amount differs from the coupon's required amount, SaveHatke creates a refund record for you automatically — you never have to file one manually.
2
Overpayment
If you paid more than required, the extra amount is refunded back to you.
refund = received − required
3
Underpayment
If you paid less than required, you get back exactly what you paid — then you can pay the correct amount or request the partial refund.
refund = amount you paid
4
Status ladder
Every refund moves through Pending → Processing → Refunded, or is marked Rejected. All amounts are server-verified and cannot be edited.
