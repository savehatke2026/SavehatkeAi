Privacy

How SaveHatke AI handles your data.

What this page stores

This build of SaveHatke AI runs entirely in your browser. Your account details, API keys, and activity are kept in your browser's local storage on this device. Nothing is transmitted to a server by this front-end.

Conversations

Messages you send to the chatbot are processed to generate a reply. In this build, replies are generated locally in the page. When you connect a model endpoint, conversation content will be sent to that endpoint — document it here before you go live.

API keys

API keys generated from the dashboard are stored locally so you can review their prefixes. Treat any key as a secret: store it in a server-side environment variable and never commit it or ship it in client-side code.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and includes no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking scripts. It loads no external fonts or assets.

Your choices

You can remove everything this site has stored at any time from the Profile page using "Delete local account data", or by clearing site data in your browser settings.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact page.
